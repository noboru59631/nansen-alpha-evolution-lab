## Buildathon form description

Nansen Alpha Evolution Lab starts where Smart Money discovery ends. It is an independent research layer that uses Nansen point-in-time data to test whether a historical signal is still alive through chronological walk-forward OOS evaluation, transaction costs, confidence intervals, crowding, and model disagreement. The lifecycle is measured as LIVE, DECAYING, or DEAD; confirmed death is then tested for NEUTRAL, INVERTED, or REBORN afterlife behavior. In a 90-day, 55-token Solana study, 51 signals were UNKNOWN, two were DECAYING, and two were DEAD. One measured PERCOLATOR case moved DEAD → INVERTED → FADE. Weak evidence produces UNKNOWN or ABSTAIN rather than a forced BUY or fabricated win rate. Alpha death does not automatically mean contrarian alpha. Saved Verified Research is clearly separated from LIVE Nansen data, which is loaded only through a server-side proxy; the API key never enters the browser. Powered by Nansen; independent project; not a trading bot or official Nansen product.
